Oats With Soya Milk

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats (portion up to discretion)
  • Vemondo soya milk (ratio of milk to oats up to discretion)
  • 1 tspn ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbspn ground flaxseed
  • 1 tbspn smooth peanut butter
  • (Optional) 1 banana or 1 serving plain yoghurt

Equipment

  • Bowl
  • Teaspoon
  • Tablespoon

Steps

  1. Pour the rolled oats into the bowl
  2. Add 1 tspn of ground cinnamon
  3. Add 1 tbspn ground flaxseed
  4. Add 1 tbspn smooth peanut butter (add this last, so you won't have to wash the spoon)
  5. Use the teaspoon to scrape the excess peanut butter off the tablespoon
  6. Add soya milk to the bowl
  7. Stir well

Notes

This is to be the default breafkast meal.

The oats should be rolled, with nothing added.

The Vemondo soya milk should be fortified with Vitamin D, Riboflavin, Vitamin B12, Calcium, and Iodine. Either the refrigerated or the non-refrigerated option may be chosen.

The flaxseed should be ground, for better digestibility.

The peanut butter should be 100% peanut.
