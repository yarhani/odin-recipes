Lentils and Mixed Veg, Ginger and Chilli

Back to Homepage

Ingredients

  • 70g Thai Jasmine rice (vary portion size at own discretion)
  • 65g Red lentils (vary portion size at own discretion)
  • 250g frozen Freshona mixed country vegetables
  • Sprinkling of ground ginger
  • Sprinkling of chilli powder
  • (Optional) 1 banana or 1 serving plain yoghurt

Equipment

  • Midea MB-12X1-306E Rice Cooker
  • Serving spoon
  • Bowl
  • Tablespoon
