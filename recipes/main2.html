Lentils and Mixed Veg, Garlic and Mustard

Back to Homepage

Note: images aren't quite accurate as they show an older recipe. I'll update them when I get around to it

Ingredients

  • 70g Thai Jasmine rice (vary portion size at own discretion)
  • 65g Red lentils (vary portion size at own discretion)
  • 250g frozen Freshona mixed country vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, carrots, peas)
  • Sprinkling of ground garlic
  • 1 tspn of Dijon mustard
  • (Optional) 1 banana or 1 serving plain yoghurt
  • (Optional) Glass of water of Vermondo soya milk

Equipment

  • Midea MB-12X1-306E Rice Cooker
  • Serving spoon
  • 1 chopstick
  • Bowl
  • Tablespoon

Steps

  1. Pour the rice into the rice cooker bowl
  2. Pour the lentils into the rice cooker bowl
  3. Add water up to about halfway between the 0.5 and 1 marks in the rice cooker bowl
  4. Add the frozen vegetables
  5. Add a sprinkling of ground garlic
  6. Stir the bowl with the chopstick
  7. Start the rice cooker on the default setting (30 min, the top left option)
  8. When the rice cooker is done, serve
  9. Mix in 1 tspn of mustard, then enjoy

Notes

Serves 1

This is to be the main alternative meal for lunch and dinner.

Make sure to not scrape the rice cooker bowl with the metal spoon.
